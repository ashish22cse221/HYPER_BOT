04 How it works

Wire. Flash.
Pair. Done.

Open the build guide →
I · WIRE    II · FLASH    III · PAIR

Every empire started with a few stones. Yours starts with four wires, one click and a four-digit code — then the bot carries the rest.

Time≈ 15 min
01

Wire it

An ESP32-C3 board and a tiny OLED. Four wires: power, ground and two data lines. No soldering needed with jumper wires.

02

Flash it

Plug it into your laptop, open the build page in Chrome or Edge and click Install. No coding tools.

03

Pair it

The bot shows a 4-digit code on its face. Type it in the app. It remembers your phone forever.
