01 Parts

What you need.

Cheap, common parts. If you have a breadboard and jumper wires lying around, you're already halfway there.

C3
Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3The brain: Bluetooth + Wi-Fi, USB-C. Other ESP32-C3 boards work if you match the pins.
OL
0.96″ OLED · SSD1306128×64, I2C, 4 pins (GND VCC SCL SDA). The face.
USB
USB-C data cableMust carry data, not just charge. #1 cause of "board not found".
4×
Jumper wiresFemale-to-female, or solder short wires if you're building it into a case.
PC
A laptopWindows, Mac or Linux with Chrome or Edge, for the one-click installer.
APP
An Android phoneAndroid 8 or newer with Bluetooth LE, for the HyperBot app.
BZ
Passive buzzer · optionalFor little beeps. Goes on D0.
BOX
A case · optional3D-print one, use a tiny box, or leave it naked. Brutalism.
02 Wiring

Four wires.

Power and ground, plus the two I2C data lines. Do this with the board unplugged.

GroundGNDGND—

Buzzer (optional)+ legD0GPIO 2
CHECK
Read the labels printed on your OLED. Some modules put VCC first and GND second — swapping them can kill the screen.
TIP
Use 3V3, not 5V. The OLED is happy on 3.3 V and the data lines stay at safe levels.
1.3″?
The bigger 1.3″ OLEDs use a different chip (SH1106) and show a shifted, garbled picture with this firmware. A 1.3″ build is coming — for now, use the 0.96″.
03 Install

One click.
No coding.

Your browser flashes the firmware straight onto the board over USB. Takes about a minute.

NOPE

  • Only replaces the firmware

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer to install. Open this page over https:// to install.
  1. Plug the bot into your laptopWith the USB-C data cable. The OLED can stay wired up.
  2. Click an installerChrome asks which device to use. Pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit (sometimes shown as a COM port) and hit Connect.
  3. Click Install and waitAbout a minute. Don't unplug it until it says installation complete.
  4. Watch the faceThe board restarts on its own and says hi. On to the next step.
NOT LISTED?
Unplug the board, hold the tiny BOOT button, plug it back in, then release. Click the installer again. Still nothing? Try another cable — most "missing board" problems are charge-only cables.
LOGS
After installing, the installer window has Logs & console. The bot prints what it's doing there, like [OLED] OK at 0x3C or wiring tips if the screen isn't found.
04 First boot

It says hi.

Until a phone pairs, the bot loops through a little welcome. It never shows this again after setup — power cycles don't bring it back.

0–3 S
Hello

Shows its name, like HyperBot-1A2B, and firmware version.

3–15 S
Get my app

Scan the QR with your phone to land on this site's app download.

15–45 S
Just vibing

Plays its faces so it never looks stuck, then loops back.

WHEN THE APP ASKS
Pair code

A 4-digit code for 2 minutes. 3 tries, then it makes a new one.

05 App + pair

Type the code.

  1. Get the appDownload the APK on your Android phone and open it. Android asks to allow "install unknown apps" — say yes for your browser.
  2. Tap ConnectAllow Bluetooth (and Location on Android 11 and older). Keep the bot close.
  3. Type the 4 digitsRead them off the bot's screen. The app sends them as soon as you type the fourth digit.
  4. ALL SET!The bot shows ALL SET!, the app remembers it and connects by itself next time.

07 Help

Something off?

The usual suspects, in the order they usually happen.

The installer can't find my board
Use a data USB-C cable (try another one). Hold BOOT while plugging in, then click the installer again. Close Arduino IDE / PlatformIO / any serial monitor — only one program can use the port. No driver is needed for the XIAO ESP32-C3.
The screen stays black
Open Logs & console in the installer. If it says [OLED] NOT FOUND, check VCC → 3V3, GND → GND and that SDA/SCL aren't swapped (SDA → D8, SCL → D9). The app also warns "screen not found" when it connects.
The picture is shifted or garbled
You probably have a 1.3″ OLED (SH1106 chip). This firmware drives the 0.96″ SSD1306. A 1.3″ build is on the way.
The app says "No HyperBot found"
Turn on Bluetooth, give the app the Bluetooth permission (and Location on Android 11 and older), and keep the bot within a couple of metres. If another phone is connected to it right now, disconnect that one first.
The app says "Board firmware is older than the app"
Plug the bot into a laptop and use Update firmware. Your GIFs and settings stay.
I lost my phone / it won't accept my phone
Reset the pairing without any phone: unplug and replug the bot 5 times in a row, each time within 6 seconds of powering on. The bot shows RESET DONE and setup runs again. Your GIFs stay. From a paired phone you can also use Bot tab → This bot → Reset bot.
Can I use it without the app?
Yes — it plays its faces on its own. On Wi-Fi it also has a small web dashboard at hyperbot.local; the Wi-Fi setup hotspot is called "HyperBot".
